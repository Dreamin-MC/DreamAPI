# Changelog 0.5.8

## 🗺️ Navigation & Orientation Enhancements

- **Cardinal Line Yaw Correction**:
  - Fixed a mathematical offset in `AStartPathFinder.getYaw` where straight paths (North, South, East, West) were rotated 90° sideways.
  - Waypoints and navigation entities now align perfectly along straight corridors and paths.
- **3D Pitch Targeting for Item Displays**:
  - `ItemDisplayNavigationTask` now computes both `yaw` and `pitch` directly pointing towards the next displayed item.
  - Item displays on slopes, stairs, or vertical elevation changes smoothly tilt upward or downward to face their target in full 3D space.
  - Added a zero-distance division guard in `AStartPathFinder.getPitch`.
- **Balanced & Proportional Item Spacing**:
  - Replaced naive modulo indexing with an evenly balanced distribution algorithm along the whole path length.
  - Completely eliminates clustering where the destination (`endItem`) and the previous item were glued together.
  - Dynamic realignment: modifying `spacing` at runtime via `setSpacing(int)` immediately updates both `yaw` and `pitch` on all active displays in the world.
